Comfy Reader - Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

The short version

Comfy Reader is an app for reading PDFs, books, and comics on your Android phone. It has no account and no login. Your files, bookmarks, and settings stay on your phone, and the app never uploads your documents. The free version shows small ads on the home screen, and those ads come from Google (see "Ads" below).

What stays on your phone

Comfy Reader only opens a file when you pick it, or when you open it from another app such as Files. While you read, it keeps a temporary working copy inside its own private storage and deletes that copy when you close the file. Saved on your phone, inside the app:

  • Your bookmarks (page or position and the time saved) and where you stopped reading in each file
  • A list of recently opened files, with their names
  • Your settings, such as reading light, brightness, text size, and the light bar
  • Signatures you draw to put on PDFs, saved as pictures
  • The date you first opened the app, used to count the free trial

When you add text or a signature to a PDF and save, the changes are written into the PDF file you choose, on your phone. The app is set so its data is not copied to Android cloud backups. Deleting the app deletes all of the above.

Phone permissions

Comfy Reader does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or all of your files. It reads only the files you choose through Android's file picker.

Trial and Pro

Comfy Reader includes a 14-day trial with every feature. After that, the free version keeps reading and bookmarks, and Comfy Reader Pro is a one-time purchase that removes ads and adds PDF editing and comics. Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details, only that Pro has been unlocked.

Ads

The free version shows small ads on the home screen, never while you are reading. They are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, your approximate location from your internet connection, and how you interact with the ads, under Google's own privacy policy. We do not send Google your files, bookmarks, or anything you read. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your Android settings, and Comfy Reader Pro removes the ads.

Children

Comfy Reader is a general-purpose reader and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
