Comfy Reader - Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

The short version

Comfy Reader is an app for reading PDFs, books, and comics on your Android phone. It has no account and no login. Your files, bookmarks, and settings stay on your phone, and the app never uploads your documents. Comfy Reader is free, with no ads, no purchases, and no tracking. It uses the internet only if you choose to download extra voices (see "Internet" below).

What stays on your phone

Comfy Reader only opens a file when you pick it, or when you open it from another app such as Files. While you read, it keeps a temporary working copy inside its own private storage and deletes that copy when you close the file. Saved on your phone, inside the app:

  • Your bookmarks (page or position and the time saved) and where you stopped reading in each file
  • A list of recently opened files, with their names
  • Your settings, such as reading light, brightness, text size, and the light bar
  • Signatures you draw to put on PDFs, saved as pictures
  • Your reading-aloud choices (voice, speed, pitch) and, for scanned PDFs, the text recognized from pages you listened to, kept in the app's temporary storage so a page is only read once

When you add text or a signature to a PDF and save, the changes are written into the PDF file you choose, on your phone. The app is set so its data is not copied to Android cloud backups. Deleting the app deletes all of the above.

Phone permissions

Comfy Reader does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or all of your files. It reads only the files you choose through Android's file picker.

Reading aloud

Read aloud uses the voice engine already on your phone, such as Google's or Samsung's. Scanned pages are turned into text by Google's text recognition, which runs on your phone and does not send the page anywhere. The words being read are handed to your phone's voice engine only, under that engine's own privacy policy.

Internet

Comfy Reader has no ads, no analytics, and no account, and it never uploads your files. The only thing it uses the internet for is to download extra reading voices when you ask it to. That download is a plain file download: it sends your device's internet address to the server that hosts the voice file, as any download does, and nothing about you or your files. Voices you download stay on your phone, and you can delete them from the app at any time.

Children

Comfy Reader is a general-purpose reader and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
